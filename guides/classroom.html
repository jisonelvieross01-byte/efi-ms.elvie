CIRCUITLAB · TEACHER ACTIVITY GUIDE

45 minutes of hands-on discovery.

Cambridge AS & A Level Physics, Chapter 8.1: circuit symbols and diagrams. Students identify components, build complete circuits, use meters, compare series and parallel arrangements, and explain faults.

Open CircuitLab ↗

Prepare the classroom

CircuitLab now has 25 simple questions: one teacher demo and 24 student tasks. Each has two minutes. This 45-minute introduction uses selected tasks; completing every student task at the full time allowance needs 48 minutes plus the demonstration.

0–7 min · Teacher demonstration

Open question 1. A cell, lamp and one wire are already on the board. Press Start, then drag from the free lamp terminal to the free cell terminal. The lamp lights and the correct-answer popup appears. Choose View circuit to explain the complete loop.

Show the library's IEC symbols, Rotate, the Wire tool, Undo and the 3D parts/Symbols switch. Show answer unlocks with 40 seconds left and opens the circuit picture immediately. Use Teacher Pause if you need more explanation time.

7–19 min · Cells, batteries and switches

Use questions 2–7. Students recognise a cell, battery, open switch, closed switch and resistor. Make predictions before joining wires. An open-switch diagram can be correct even though its lamp is off. There are no numerical settings or calculations to complete.

19–29 min · Meters and branches

Choose questions 8–12. Point out the variable resistor's arrow. Put A in the main path and V across a lamp. Compare one series loop with two parallel lamp branches. Ask students to explain the connections rather than calculate readings.

29–39 min · Recognise more symbols

Choose five tasks from the fuse, diode, LED, thermistor, LDR, capacitor, junction and power-supply questions. Students copy the symbol, name the part and connect the diagram. A capacitor blocks steady DC in this model. Connecting wires that cross does not join them; a junction dot does.

39–45 min · Exit ticket

Ask students to sketch a cell, switch and lamp; add A in series and V across the lamp. Label one junction and explain why an open switch stops current. No calculation is needed. Use questions 21–25 for follow-up practice.

Help and success

Hint shows a faint guide. Show answer unlocks with 40 seconds left and opens the full picture immediately, even after a timeout. There are no dances, penalties or permission steps. Correct diagrams earn a congratulations popup and unlock Next. Back is always available. Student questions start with a clean board; the teacher demo is prepared automatically.

Model notes

The solver is DC steady state. Lamps have fixed resistance, sensor values are presets, and capacitor charging is not simulated. Readings are optional and folded away; the goal is familiarity with symbols and diagrams.

Hint unlocks after 30 seconds (1 minute 30 seconds left). Show answer unlocks with 40 seconds left. Both stay available after time runs out; completed questions allow review.
