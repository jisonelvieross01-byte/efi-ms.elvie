Circuit readings

Formula guide

Q = It · charge (C), current (A), time (s)

V = IR · potential difference (V), resistance (Ω)

R = V/I · for non-zero current

P = VI · power (W)

In series: current is the same.
In parallel: potential difference is the same.
At a junction: current in = current out.

Model & controls

Ideal wires, meters and voltage sources. DC steady state only. Lamps use fixed resistance; brightness is relative to 1.8 W. No capacitor charging, AC, reverse breakdown or thermal dynamics.

Tap terminals to connect; tap a wire to select it. Select a part then rotate or delete. Double-tap switches to toggle. Keyboard: R rotate, Delete remove, Escape cancel; Ctrl/Cmd Z undo.

Orange arrows show conventional current; blue arrows show electron flow. Measurements use left-to-right terminal polarity before rotation. A negative value means the opposite direction.
