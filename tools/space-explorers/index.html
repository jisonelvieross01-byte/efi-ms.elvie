Weight & mass

W = m × g
W: weight in newtons (N)
m: mass in kilograms (kg)
g: gravity strength in N/kg

Earth 9.8 • Moon 1.6 • Jupiter 24.8 N/kg

Mass measures how much matter an object has. Moving it does not change its mass.

Orbits & motion

Gravity pulls a planet toward the Sun. Its forward motion and gravity make a curved orbit.

For circular orbits around the same Sun: farther planets move more slowly and take longer.

Tides

Tidal range = high tide − low tide.
Keep both heights in metres (m).

Spring: Sun, Earth, Moon in a line → larger range.
Neap: directions to Sun and Moon at 90° → smaller range.

Solar System formation

About 4.6 billion years ago, gravity pulled gas and dust together. A spinning disk formed. The Sun formed at the centre; small pieces joined to build planets.

Simplified models: sizes, distances, time, and tide bulges are not to scale. Real tides also depend on coasts, sea depth, and local conditions. Jupiter's value is a reference near its cloud tops; it has no solid surface.
