Help

3D parts show real objects; Symbols shows circuit diagrams.

Tap a part, then tap the board. Drag from one terminal dot to another, or tap both dots. Double-tap a switch to flip it.

Tap a wire to show its handles. Drag an end to reconnect, or the middle to stretch. Tap Rotate on a selected part to turn it 90°.

Keys: R rotate · Delete remove · Ctrl+Z undo.

Orange = current. Blue = electrons. A minus sign means the opposite direction.

Model: ideal DC only. No AC or charging.
